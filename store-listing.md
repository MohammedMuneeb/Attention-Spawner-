**Attention Spawner** is a powerful, lightweight app blocker designed to help you break free from digital distractions, reduce screen time, and stay focused on what truly matters. 
 
Whether you are studying for exams, focusing on deep work, or trying to curb mindlessly scrolling social media, Attention  Spawner puts you in full control of your phone usage. 

--- 
### 🌟 Key Features 
• **Instant App Blocking**: Select distracting apps and block them with a single tap.
• **Foreground Interception**: Displays a clean, non-intrusive focus screen whenever a blocked app is launched.
• **Custom Intercept Screens**: Stay motivated with mindfulness prompts when you open a blocked app.
• **Privacy-First & On-Device**: Your usage data and installed app lists stay 100% on your device. We do not track, collect, or store your private data on remote servers.
• **Lightweight & Battery Friendly**: Optimized background execution that won't drain your battery 

---

### 🛡️ Why Attention Spawner ? 

Unlike complex focus apps that require account sign-ups or sell your data, Attention Spawner is built with **privacy and simplicity** at its core. It is completely open-source, ad-free, and works entirely offline on your phone 

---

### 🔒 Permissions & Privacy Transparency 

Attention Spawner requires specific Android permissions to function effectively as an app blocker:

- **App Usage Stats (`PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS`)**: To detect when a blocked app is opened.
- **Display Over Apps (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`)**: To present the intercept screen over blocked applications.
- **Query All Packages (`QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`)**: To allow you to pick and configure which apps you want to restrict.

*All permission checks and app monitoring are processed strictly on your local device *
